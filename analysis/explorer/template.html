Showdown Card Values

Every card scored by the exact dice math against the rest of its pool. Value is runs added per game over an average card. Bargain is how far that beats a typical card at the same points. Tiers rank bargains within hitters, starters and relievers.

Model, not game results

The math doesn't count stolen bases, catcher arm or a player's own glove yet, and it ignores icons. So fast runners, strong-armed catchers and slick fielders play better than they score here. Usage and win rates need showdown2000.fun's game logs, which only its owners can share.

Points vs value

How it's scored: each half-inning is solved exactly as a Markov chain over the 24 base/out states, with optimal extra-base decisions. Hitters face the pool's pitchers weighted by innings; pitchers face the pool's average lineup. The league is every card with at least 250 plate appearances or 30 innings that season (Showdown Bot's own sample-size flag).

Bargain = value minus the straight-line fit of value on points within hitters, starters or relievers. Cards at the minimum price sort last under "Worst bargain", since they can't get cheaper. Tiers: S top 5%, A next 15%, B next 30%, C next 30%, D bottom 20%.

Cards: Showdown Bot (showdownbot.com) card database, downloaded 2026-11-07; WotC 2000 set from the birdman093 and digitgopher fan datasets.
